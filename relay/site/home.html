Private by design

Every session is encrypted from one computer to the other. Nobody in between — including us — can see your screen.

End-to-end encryption

Each session uses fresh X25519 keys and ChaCha20-Poly1305, so traffic is private, tamper-proof and can't be replayed.

Your password never travels

The viewer proves it knows the password with a cryptographic signature. The password itself is never sent — not even encrypted.

Identity checks

Every computer has its own identity key. You confirm it the first time, and TetherDesk warns you if it ever changes.

Built-in brute-force protection

Repeated wrong passwords lock out the guesser, with longer lockouts each time.

Questions

How do I reach my computer when I'm away?

Install TetherDesk on it, open Share this PC and tick Always on. It stays reachable by its ID, starts sharing when it boots, and restarts sharing automatically if anything goes wrong. Then connect by that ID from the app or any browser.

Do I need port forwarding, a VPN, or router changes?

No. Both computers connect outwards to the TetherDesk relay, which pairs them by ID. It works behind home routers, most firewalls and mobile hotspots.

Can the relay see my screen?

No. Sessions are end-to-end encrypted between the two computers. The relay only forwards encrypted data it cannot read.

Does it work with more than one monitor?

Yes. When the other computer has several screens, the connection bar shows a button for each one — or press F7 to go to the next.

Why does my Mac ask for Screen Recording and Accessibility?

macOS requires your permission before any app can see or control the screen. Allow TetherDesk once under System Settings → Privacy & Security; the app has buttons that take you there.

I'm helping someone. Do they need to install TetherDesk?

No. Send them QuickSupport — a small separate app that runs without installing. They read you the ID and one-time password, and closing it ends the session.

What does it cost?

Nothing. TetherDesk is free to download and use.
